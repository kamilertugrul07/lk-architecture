The Stages
of Your Project

A structured and tailored approach, from the first conversation to the delivery of your project, to create an interior that truly reflects you.

  1. 01

    Initial Meeting

    A discussion about your ideas, needs, lifestyle, preferred style, budget and the key directions of the project.

    Following this meeting, a preliminary contract is signed.

    Initial payment: 40% of the project fee

  2. 02

    Site Measurement

    On-site measurements, photo and video recording of the space.

    This step provides the basis for the space planning.

  3. 03

    Project Study

    Development of 2 to 3 layout options, based on the potential of the space, its technical constraints and your requirements.

    Analysis and selection of the most suitable solution.

    A meeting is then held to review the options and choose one.

    At the same time, the style, colour palette, materials, finishes and key design ideas are discussed.

    Please prepare 2 to 4 images for each room as inspiration.

    This stage represents 10% of the project.

  4. 04

    Detailed Mood Board

    Development of a detailed mood board, according to the selected package:

    • overall atmosphere with proposed materials and furniture, or
    • a more precise selection with specific references from real suppliers.

    The mood board is first presented for the main living areas (kitchen and living room, or open-plan kitchen-living room) and all bedrooms.

    A meeting is then held to review and confirm the proposed style.

    The work then continues for the bathrooms, hallways, dressing rooms and all other areas of the home, followed by a final review meeting.

    Payment: 40% of the project fee

  5. 05

    Technical Documentation

    Preparation of the complete technical documentation required for the project’s implementation.

    Once all details are confirmed, the project is 80% complete.

  6. 06

    Final Drawings

    If needed, a final meeting is arranged to confirm specific details, such as: flooring layout, material choices, multi-level ceilings, the number and placement of electrical outlets, light fixtures, etc.

    Final payment: 20% of the project fee

  7. 07

    Your Project, Ready to Be Realised

    Delivery of the complete design package, ready for implementation.

    You may, of course, carry out the works on your own, but you can also entrust the implementation to our team of professionals, who ensure coordination, execution quality and full alignment with the project.

  • Estimated timeframe

    Layout proposals: adapted to the complexity of the project.

    The overall project duration depends on the size, complexity and selected scope of services.

  • Payment terms

    40% – upon contract signature

    40% – after validation of the mood board

    20% – upon final delivery of the drawings

  • Outcome

    A complete, coherent and detailed project, ready for implementation, with the freedom to realise it independently or with our full support.
