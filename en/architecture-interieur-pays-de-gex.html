Based in Cessy, LK Architecture designs made-to-measure interiors for houses and apartments across the Pays de Gex, between the Jura and Lake Geneva.

Renovation, redesign or decoration: every project starts from the way you live — for a place that resembles no one but you.

  1. 01

    Advice & concept

    Listening to what you want: space optimisation, decorative concept and moodboards, for a clear direction before any decision.

  2. 02

    Plans & 3D visuals

    Layout plans, furniture plans and 3D visuals: see your interior before it exists.

  3. 03

    Materials & follow-up

    Materials, colours, furniture and lighting, shopping lists, choice of craftspeople and aesthetic guidance throughout the works.
