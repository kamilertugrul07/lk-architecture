Based in Cessy, a few minutes from Geneva, LK Architecture helps private clients across the canton create singular interiors — apartments, houses and villas — from the first idea to the very last detail.

Every project is made to measure, starting from the way you live — for a place that resembles no one but you.

  1. 01

    Advice & concept

    Listening to what you want: space optimisation, decorative concept and moodboards, for a clear direction before any decision.

  2. 02

    Plans & 3D visuals

    Layout plans, furniture plans and 3D visuals: see your interior before it exists.

  3. 03

    Materials & follow-up

    Materials, colours, furniture and lighting, shopping lists, choice of craftspeople and aesthetic guidance throughout the works.
