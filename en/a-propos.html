About Me

Founder of LK Architecture, Liliya K. has nurtured a passion for art and interior architecture since childhood. After completing an artistic education, she went on to study interior architecture at university, where she developed a keen eye for volumes, proportions, composition and light.

Her vision of space is holistic. She is not driven by the simple arrangement of beautiful objects, but by the desire to reveal the balance of a place, understand its potential and give each element its rightful place. This approach is shaped by both artistic sensitivity and hands-on expertise in bespoke layouts, materials and technical constraints.

Throughout her journey, her professional and personal experiences have deepened her understanding of spaces and the people who live in them. They have taught her that an interior is never just about appearance: it must support daily habits, meet real needs and provide a sense of comfort that is felt every day.

Through LK Architecture, she creates unique spaces where aesthetics and functionality come together naturally. Each project begins with careful listening and a genuine understanding of the people who will live there. The choices of volumes, materials and details then take on their full meaning: to create an interior that truly reflects them, where they feel completely at home, and whose beauty stands the test of time.

A place only becomes a true home
when it finds its place in the lives of those who live in it.
